# Privacy Policy

**Black Hole Lab** · Effective 5 October 2026

Black Hole Lab collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no
analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it sends
nothing about you or your use of it to the developer or to anyone else.

## Network access

Black Hole Lab connects to the internet for one purpose only: to download NASA's
star map, which the Look Around feature needs. The download happens only after
you press **OK** in the dialog that asks "Download missing dependencies?", and
only once; the map is kept for later views.

The map comes from NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio
([svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851)). As with any
download, NASA's server receives your IP address and an ordinary web request.
NASA's handling of that is described in the [NASA Web Privacy
Policy](https://www.nasa.gov/privacy/). Black Hole Lab sends nothing else with
the request.

## Files on your computer

Black Hole Lab writes files only on your computer, and only these:

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Saves you make | Wherever you choose in the save dialog |
| A safety copy of the current run, made when you load a save over it | `autosave-before-load.bhl` in an app data folder |
| The star map, after you agree to the download | `maps` in an app data folder |
| Look Around photographs, each with the save it was made from | `Black Hole Lab views` in your Videos folder |
| Working files while a view is being made | Your temporary folder; removed when the view is finished |

None of these files leave your computer unless you share them yourself.

The app's data folders are the usual per-user places for application data on
each system (on Windows, under `AppData`). When the Microsoft Store version is
uninstalled, Windows removes them, star map included. Your saves and your Look
Around photographs stay where they are.

## Children

Black Hole Lab collects no information from anyone, including children.
